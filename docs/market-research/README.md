# Smart Mirror Market Research

## Objective
The purpose of this survey is to understand what potential users would want from a Smart Mirror AI assistant. We aim to understand what would be the most useful features and how users would prefer to interact with this type of system.

## Method
Our survey uses open ended questions to avoid leading responses as recommended by our advisor, Dr. Courand. The questions were written in a way to encourage thoughtful feed back from potential users.
