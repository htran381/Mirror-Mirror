# Market Research Results

# Overview
We conducted a market research survey for our Mirror Mirror project to better understand what potential users would want from a smart mirror assistant, how they would prefer to interact with it, and which features would be most useful in daily life. The goal of this research was to guide our design decisions using real user input rather than assumptions.

# Main Findings

1. Users want practical daily assistance
A common trend among responses was that users were most interested in features that could support their everyday routines. The most frequently mentioned needs included:
- weather updates
- calendar reminders
- task lists or to-do support
- quick daily summaries
- traffic or commute information


2. Personalization is important
Many responses showed that users would want the mirror to feel personalized to their schedule, preferences, and habits. Users seemed more interested in a system that could adapt to them rather than display the same information for everyone.

Examples of desired personalization included:
- customized module placement
- personalized reminders
- different information depending on the time of day
- user-specific profiles


3. Users prefer simple and natural interaction
Responses suggested that users would prefer interaction that feels natural and low effort. Many participants seemed to favor voice interaction, quick visual updates, or a combination of both, rather than complex manual input.


4. Users want the system to save time, not create distraction
Another repeated idea was that the smart mirror should be efficient and supportive without becoming overwhelming. Users appeared to prefer concise, relevant information that helps them prepare for the day rather than a cluttered display.


# Recurring Themes
Across the responses, several themes appeared consistently:
- convenience
- personalization
- efficiency
- ease of use
- support for morning or daily routines


# Conclusion
Overall, the survey results suggest that users are primarily looking for a smart mirror that supports their daily routines in a practical and efficient way. Users value quick access to essential information such as weather, reminders, schedules, and traffic. The responses also highlight the importance of personalization. Users want a system that can adapt to their habits, preferences, and daily patterns. This reinforces our decision to explore profile-based features and dynamic content tailored to each user. Additionally, the preference for simple and natural interaction indicates that the system should remain easy to use, especially during busy morning routines. While some users mentioned features like outfit suggestions or rating their appearance, these fall outside the scope of our current focus, as we aim to prioritize practical and personalized support for everyday routines.
